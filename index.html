The problem · Question 3

Agents read far more than they need

Over-reading

Whole files for one function

Re-reading

Same file, paid for again and again

Bloated history

Old turns, logs and traces resent every call

Does shorter context mean worse answers?

Models can lose facts buried in the middle of long contexts (Liu et al., 2024).

The gap

Existing tools claim 60–90% savings from tracking estimates, with no published controlled quality study (LeanCTX).
The idea

Meet Lens: it focuses the agent on what matters

Goal: cut tokens and API cost for AI coding agents, report the energy and CO₂ that follows, and prove what compression does to answer quality.

03

Cache + diff

Never resends a file; sends only what changed
04

History & tool output

Summarizes old turns, shrinks logs and traces

Secrets redaction
